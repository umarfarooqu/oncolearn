Breast Cancer: Key Statistics

Breast cancer is one of the most common cancers worldwide, but early detection and careful awareness make it one of the most treatable. These facts show why knowledge and preventive action are essential.

2.3M
new breast cancer cases every year globally
685K
lives lost annually to breast cancer
1 in 8
women will face breast cancer in their lifetime
90%
survival rate when breast cancer is caught early

Early detection is the strongest advantage

Regular self-exams, annual screening, and prompt follow-up for changes can dramatically improve outcomes. OncoLearn helps you stay informed so you can act sooner.

Awareness matters for everyone

Breast cancer mostly affects women, but men can also be diagnosed. This makes clear, inclusive education and easy-to-use prediction tools important for all users.
